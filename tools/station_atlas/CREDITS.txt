Station art credits (BurnMon Dev, the Station secret screen)
=============================================================

Every sprite in cmd/burnmon-dev/station/station_atlas.js (space theme) and
station_atlas_site.js (site theme) comes from the sources below. All of them are
CC0 1.0 (public domain, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No CC-BY item is used, so no credit line is required by a licence. Crediting
the authors is a courtesy and is done here.

Checked 2026-10-02. The downloaded kits live in tools/station_atlas/kits/
(gitignored); render_site.py records the kit files it renders in
kits/_renders_site/sources.json.
